# Might assessments: late-mid-game reference v3

All **138 definitions** (39 spells, 61 items, 16 passives, 22 enemies) have been reviewed. **Seven values change and 131 are retained after review.** Ratings remain authored **Estimated** judgments; the Library derives tiers through the unchanged shared promotion rule. ## Late-mid-game reference v3

Level **31**, **160 base points**: 40 starting points plus 30 level-ups at four points each; cumulative nominal XP to reach 31 is 46,500 under the current curve. This is a design benchmark, not an observed player percentile or an implemented level cap. The previous level-41 budget needed 82,000 nominal XP. All roles use equal attribute budgets and the same access to E–B gear, with no A/S equipment or synthetic stat allowance. The accessory mix contains one E, two D, one C and three B pieces from actual enemy drops. This represents an established but unfinished loadout. It is not a claim that individual gear Might values sum to equal character power.

Development-granted advanced spells remain individually assessed on these character builds; they are not assumed in the baseline kits.

Apply all gear through normal BM preparation. Gear INT/VIT affect regeneration and scaling, while maximum mana/HP remain based on persisted base attributes. Current tactical health regeneration is **0.375 × effective VIT**, replacing the obsolete 0.5 assumption in the v2 rationale. The agility profile uses an equal-budget specialization for AGI-scaling content; weapon comparisons fix Iron Cuirass for every role. A supplemental healing sequence equips Nature’s Embrace. No passive is bundled in baseline heroes.

Enemy comparisons use the physical/caster party above, transferring one caster base point from AGI to STR (STR21/INT60/VIT55/AGI24) to fix initiative order while retaining the 160-point budget. Effective caster STR21/AGI44 differs; HP, mana, defenses and caster scaling stay as listed.

Use 6/12-round horizons, fresh or 55%-HP/10%-mana starts, 0 or 30 armor/20 MR, about 1.5 useful local and two global enemy targets and one or two allies. Level-41 v2 inputs remain a sensitivity in the separately retained fresh-baseline evidence.

## Why the seven numeric changes

### Bladestorm Rhythm: 320 → 600

Increase 320 → 600: the previous rating used AGI30 instead of a compatible agility build. The level-31, 160-point AGI60 build reaches AGI88 with real E–B equipment and 20% crit, producing 205.44/145.44 preview damage across two immediate strikes. Actual sequence pairs confirm strong added output. Price below the 877-point direct-damage comparison after melee, overkill, 15 mana and cooldown 4. Two immediate strikes consume one activation and have no charging discount. This is a build-selection correction, not a general catalogue rescale.

Source: [bladestorm-rhythm](../../apps/game/src/spells/bladestorm-rythm.ts); preview and sequence evidence above.

### Volt Lash: 1250 → 1150

Reduce 1250 → 1150: four INT82 hits total about 265.22/185.22 versus the former 302.7/222.7. Every hit can stun and can choose a fresh living target; discount overlapping denial and overkill. Real sequences verify all four hits and control; 35 mana and cooldown 4. The modest 8% correction preserves its leading burst rating.

Source: [volt-lash](../../apps/game/src/spells/volt-lash.ts); preview and sequence evidence above.

### Vital Wellspring: 420 → 340

Reduce 420 → 340: the old rationale used 0.5 × VIT regeneration and quoted 82.32 mean preserved HP. Current tactical characters use 0.375 × VIT: effective VIT61/86 recovers 23/32 after rounding, or 29/40 with this passive (+6/+8 instead of the old +9/+13). Fresh cases below confirm lower sustained recovery. Retain a substantial action-free sustain premium while correcting the obsolete basis; do not also add healing to end health. Canonical 256 pairs: mean remaining-HP change 28.5, damage change 6.27, explicit-healing change 0.63, deaths 128 → 128. Supplemental 256 pairs: remaining-HP change 61.24, damage change 7, explicit-healing change -0.09, deaths 23 → 2. These endpoints overlap and are not added into a Might formula; see each artifact’s distinct activation conditions.

Source: [vital-wellspring](../../apps/game/src/passive-skills/vital-wellspring.passive.ts); paired canonical and supplemental passive evidence above.

### Arcane Channeling: 780 → 720

Reduce 780 → 720: INT82 releases 155.8 raw damage per surviving original target, preview 174.50/154.50 after crit/defense. Two useful targets average 329 damage across the cast plus one blocked activation, about 164.5 per committed action. Immediate-payoff pairs verify the lost action; target-loss and caster-death cases reduce or cancel the release. Apply the remaining modest discount for delay, risk, 40 mana and cooldown 6 once. Charging is a restriction, never a unique-effect premium.

Source: [arcane-channeling](../../apps/game/src/spells/arcane-channeling.ts); preview and sequence evidence above.

### Final Verdict: 410 → 380

Reduce 410 → 380: STR66 gives about 82.66/52.66 ordinary damage versus 98.8/68.8. The ≤10%-HP branch substitutes maximum health before defense, so retain a conditional finisher premium only for useful extra remaining HP. Melee, 10 mana, cooldown 2; do not value uncapped overkill.

Source: [final-verdict](../../apps/game/src/spells/final-verdict.ts); preview and sequence evidence above.

### Precise Thrust: 55 → 85

Increase 55 → 85: review on the compatible, equal-budget AGI88 build instead of AGI30. Preview rises from about 14.9/0 to 30.12/4.04 per covered enemy. Two-tile alignment, flat armor, 5 mana and cooldown 1 keep the correction well below a direct proportional rescale.

Source: [precise-thrust](../../apps/game/src/spells/precise-thrust.ts); preview and sequence evidence above.

### Tidepiercer Thrust: 460 → 430

Reduce 460 → 430: STR66 gives 69.55/41.80 per line target versus about 81.5/53.8 previously. Keep the distinct three-tile reach and 30% chance to ignore 25% defense; favorable line trials support retaining most of the rating. About 1.5 useful targets, 25 mana, cooldown 3.

Source: [tidepiercer-thrust](../../apps/game/src/spells/tidepiercer-thrust.ts); preview and sequence evidence above.

Canonical enemy fights all finished as party wins, with no hero deaths. Many early enemies died before acting; their ratings therefore retain explicit intrinsic durability/kit comparisons. Supplemental passive cases use 45 raw pressure and deliberately enable low-health/finishing conditions; their activation rate is not generalized. Equipment remains provisional despite fresh slot trials: limited pressure mixes and layouts do not fully sample avoidance or distinguish every second movement tile. The shared heuristic AI can underuse support. Scripted spell formations are controlled coverage cases, not observed target probabilities.

End health already includes recovery and prevention; do not add those again. Unused mana and overkill are excluded from useful output. Matching seeds do not synchronize later RNG when paths diverge. Three-round sequence endpoint differences can reflect survival and changed actions, so they are not automatic Might arithmetic. Charge risk cases intentionally remove a caster or original target; they are conditional mechanic tests, not a frequency estimate.

Implementation discrepancies remain inputs: Aegis supplies shields but no advertised defenses; Deflecting Stance subtracts actual reflection and has no independent 50% reduction; Iron Will removes DEBUFF only; Ocean Blessing’s text uses roll 5 for its upper endpoint even though actual healing uses the full roll; character mana upkeep currently routes through healing hooks. No gameplay fixes are included.
