# Battle enemy assets

These models are selected by enemy type in live and recorded battles. Related enemies share art bases. The collection contains 14 CC0 source models and two original project-owned drafts (Moss Golem and Water Elemental).

See the [complete mapping, sources, licensing, conversion details and validation](../../../../../docs/enemy-models/README.md).

- `source-manifest.json`: original glTF/GLB URLs and expected input hashes.
- `build-report.json`: prepared source models, retained clips and output hashes.
- `fbx-report.json`: zombie/treant inputs, texture hashes and converted outputs.
- `original-report.json`: original models, clips, sizes and output hashes.
- Adjacent license files preserve the KayKit, Mesh2Motion and Quaternius notices. The Tennessippi pack's creator states CC0 on its [source page](https://tennessippistudios.itch.io/treant-pack); its archive contains no separate license document.

The original project-owned models are not covered by the adjacent third-party CC0 notices. No new dedication or external publication of those drafts is implied.
